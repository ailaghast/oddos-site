Capua, 79 AD. Your father built his gladiator school from nothing, and left it with even less. Now you're a young lanista with fighters to feed and a senator who expects his money on time. In 60 days, Rome's Grand Games begin at the Flavian Amphitheatre. Turn your inheritance into the greatest gladiator school in Rome, or lose it trying.

Gladiatoria combines gladiator management with real-time arena combat. Recruit and train your fighters, decide how to spend your coin, and send your school's finest into the sand, where they fight on their own. Let their training do the talking, or take control yourself.

Fighters with baggage

Every gladiator has their own name, origin and traits. Shape recruits into champions across 10 fighting styles, sell them, or bury them. Death is permanent, and every grave gets an honest epitaph: "Never won a bout. Admirably consistent."

Run the school

Give daily orders, upgrade your buildings, and balance food, morale and coin. Navigate scheming rivals and difficult choices while keeping enough aside for the senator.

Rule the arena

Your gladiators fight for themselves, guided by your training. Watch them chain combos, parry and throw sand through duels, team fights and beast hunts, or take control of one fighter at any time and hand it back whenever you like. Win the crowd, then decide who deserves mercy. The crowd will have opinions.

Dice Dice Battles is a roguelike about lining up dice into ridiculous combos. The goddess Fortuna swallowed all the world's luck, then shattered. Pick your champion, plan your lineup at your own pace, and descend into her cursed dungeon to bring luck back to the world!

Your dice roll from left to right, activating one after another to attack enemies and defend you. Some dice power up their neighbors, some repeat them, and some copy them. Put the right dice side by side and a single roll snowballs into huge numbers.

Between battles, buy new dice, charms, and upgrades. Merge matching dice into stronger versions and group dice families for bonuses. Then get your hands dirty: stamp your dice with new traits, switch their family, or notch and enchant their faces.

But the dungeon doesn't play fair. Its monsters curse your dice, jam them, and swallow them whole. Fight through 3 acts to face Fortuna, the Broken, and return luck to the world. Good luck!

Survive the campaign

Every win earns a trophy; every loss costs a life. Collect 15 trophies before your 5 lives run out. Each shop visit is a chance to turn things around.

Rebuild the goddess

Every run earns shards that restore Fortuna at the Shrine, unlocking new champions, dice, charms, and cosmetic die finishes along the way.

Find your next challenge

Try a shared Daily Run, take a winning build into Endless to see how deep it can go, or unlock tougher runs with 10 Heresy difficulty levels.

Duel other players in async PvP

In Rivals, you face other players' lineups from the same round, their best ideas against yours. Beat them and take the crown!

    Gladiatoria · Nov 13, 2026

    Hi there! We're Odd Pixel Labs, and Gladiatoria is our game about running a gladiator school. You have 60 days to make it the best school in Rome, with a pile of debts and a senator who wants his money. Here's how it goes.

    New men go on sale every morning, from cheap condemned criminals to veterans. Each one is his own man: one never gets tired, another runs when he's hurt, a third lives for the crowd.

    There's one fight a day, rated from one star to five. As your trainer Castor likes to say: "Five stars is how men end up carved on the wall." Pick who fights and send them into the arena. They fight on their own, guided by the style, training and gear you gave them. If you can't help yourself, take control of one of your men, and hand him back whenever you like.

    Win, and your beaten opponent kneels. Spare him or finish him, while the crowd shouts its opinion. Give them what they want, and they'll love you for it. When it's your man on his knees, it's out of your hands.

    In between fights, train your men, feed them, and keep enough silver for the senator's collector. Those who die get their name carved into the wall, with an epitaph to match the life they lived.

    Those who survive might just reach the Grand Games in Rome on day 60, where Flamma the Unbroken awaits.
