SHORT DESCRIPTION

Buy dice, line them up, and watch them play. Move one die and the whole line changes. A roguelike dice battler about finding absurd combos, then testing them against a cursed dungeon or other players in async PvP.

ABOUT THE GAME

Dice Dice Battles is a roguelike about lining up dice into ridiculous combos. The goddess Fortuna swallowed all the world’s luck, then shattered. Pick your champion, plan your lineup at your own pace, and descend into her cursed dungeon to bring luck back to the world!

Your dice roll from left to right, activating one after another to attack enemies and defend you. Some dice power up their neighbors, some repeat them, and some copy them. Put the right dice side by side and a single roll snowballs into huge numbers.

Between battles, buy new dice, charms, and upgrades. Merge matching dice into stronger versions and group dice families for bonuses. Then get your hands dirty: stamp your dice with new traits, switch their family, or notch and enchant their faces.

But the dungeon doesn’t play fair. Its monsters curse your dice, jam them, and swallow them whole. Fight through 3 acts to face Fortuna, the Broken, and return luck to the world. Good luck!

- Survive the campaign: Every win earns a trophy; every loss costs a life. Collect 15 trophies before your 5 lives run out. Each shop visit is a chance to turn things around.
- Rebuild the goddess: Every run earns shards that restore Fortuna at the Shrine, unlocking new champions, dice, charms, and cosmetic die finishes along the way.
- Find your next challenge: Try a shared Daily Run, take a winning build into Endless to see how deep it can go, or unlock tougher runs with 10 Heresy difficulty levels.
- Duel other players in async PvP: In Rivals, you face other players' lineups from the same round, their best ideas against yours. Beat them and take the crown!

THE DEMO

The free demo has every mode: the campaign, Rivals (async PvP), the Daily Run and Endless. Like the full game, it's playable in all eight languages.
